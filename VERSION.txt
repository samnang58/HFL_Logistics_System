HFL Logistics ERP - Container Receiving & Tracking Separation Update
Version: 2026.09.26-v3

Key changes:
- Container Number, HFL Tracking, and China Delivery Tracking are separate identifiers.
- HFL Tracking is generated only when China warehouse physical receipt is confirmed.
- China Delivery Tracking is preserved in china_waybill_number.
- Container search supports Container / HFL Tracking / China Delivery Tracking / HFL Package Code / Package ID.
- Container movement includes Received after Cambodia Warehouse.
- Container UI explicitly labels HFL and China tracking numbers.
- Legacy mock China tracking values are migrated out of the HFL tracking field.
